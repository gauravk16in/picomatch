  ulps.** `crates/pmx-core/examples/c0probe.rs:44` read `maxLength` through serde_json
  `Value::as_f64`; serde_json 1.0.151 *without* `float_roundtrip` is not correctly

  `parse('a', {maxLength: -3.289270972837596e-293})`:

## Proposed AGENTS.md/rulebook addition

When a JS number must cross a text boundary (corpus JSON, fixtures, JSONL probes, and the §4b `pmx --serve` adapter), transport it as a decimal string (`String(v)` / `"num:"` sentinel) and decode with a correctly-rounded parser (Rust `str::parse::<f64>()` or serde_json's `float_roundtrip` feature) — serde_json's default f64 decode is *not* correctly rounded (~30% of random doubles shift 1 ulp, silently corrupting byte-exact comparisons like `InputTooLong` messages).
